# Business Rules

## Booking Rules

### 1. Availability & Conflicts
- Equipment/service provider cannot have overlapping bookings
- Bookings must be within service provider's specified availability
- Cancellation notice period: 24 hours (no penalty if cancelled within 24 hours)

### 2. Pricing
- Service provider sets base rate
- Rates can vary by: time (seasonal), distance, duration
- Discounts allowed: up to 20% for regular customers (provider discretion)
- Emergency bookings (< 6 hours notice): +25% surcharge

### 3. Booking Lifecycle
- **Pending**: Awaiting provider confirmation
- **Confirmed**: Provider accepted, payment completed
- **In Progress**: Work started
- **Completed**: Work finished, awaiting payment release
- **Cancelled**: Cancelled by either party with reason

---

## Payment Rules

### 1. Payment Terms
- 50% deposit at booking confirmation
- 50% payment on work completion
- Exception: Same-day bookings require full prepayment

### 2. Commission Structure
- RuralConnect commission: 15% of transaction value
- Paid by service provider/seller
- Commission waived for first 10 transactions (promotional period)

### 3. Refunds & Disputes
- Refund initiated within 48 hours of request
- Dispute period: 7 days after completion
- RuralConnect team mediates if dispute unresolved
- Service provider has 24 hours to respond to dispute

### 4. Payment Methods
- UPI (primary for India)
- Bank transfer
- Digital wallet
- Future: Credit/debit cards

## Rating & Review Rules

### 1. Rating Criteria
- **Quality of Work**: 1-5 stars
- **Punctuality**: 1-5 stars
- **Professionalism**: 1-5 stars
- **Communication**: 1-5 stars
- **Overall Rating**: Average of above

### 2. Review Guidelines
- Reviews must be submitted within 7 days of completion
- Text reviews: 10-500 characters
- Photos: Up to 3 images
- No abusive language, personal information, or competitor references
- Ratings < 3 stars require explanation

### 3. Consequences
- 3 consecutive ratings < 2 stars: Warning
- 5 consecutive ratings < 2 stars: Suspended
- Multiple fraud/complaint reports: Account deactivated

---

## Verification & Trust Rules

### 1. Document Verification
- All users: Aadhaar identity verification
- Tractor Owners: Vehicle RC, Insurance
- Contractors: Business registration, License
- Suppliers: GST registration, Business proof

### 2. Account Status Levels
- **Unverified**: Limited booking (max 3)
- **Verified**: Full access
- **Gold**: 50+ ratings, avg 4.5+ stars (badge, priority support)
- **Platinum**: 100+ ratings, avg 4.7+ stars (premium features)

### 3. Trust Scoring
- Based on: Ratings, reviews, response time, completion rate
- Lower score → reduced visibility in search
- Score < 2.5: Requires verification re-check

## Cancellation Rules

### 1. Farmer/Customer Cancellation
- **> 24 hours before**: Full refund
- **12-24 hours before**: 75% refund
- **6-12 hours before**: 50% refund
- **< 6 hours before**: 25% refund

### 2. Service Provider Cancellation
- **> 24 hours before**: No penalty
- **12-24 hours before**: 10% of booking amount held
- **< 12 hours before**: 25% of booking amount held + warning

### 3. RuralConnect-Initiated Cancellation
- Fraud detected, suspended users, policy violation
- Full refund to customer, account consequences for provider

---

## Dispute Resolution

### 1. Dispute Categories
- Quality not as described
- Work not completed
- Excessive charges
- Non-payment
- Safety/conduct issues

### 2. Resolution Process
- Both parties submit evidence (photos, messages, work logs)
- RuralConnect team reviews (48-72 hours)
- Mediation attempt
- Final decision if mediation fails
- Appeal allowed within 7 days

### 3. Outcomes
- Refund to customer
- Penalty/fine to provider
- Service suspension
- Account termination (severe cases)

---

## Safety & Conduct Rules

### 1. Prohibited Behavior
- Harassment or discrimination
- Sharing personal information outside platform
- Arranging payment outside platform
- Misrepresenting services/qualifications
- Operating under false identity

### 2. Consequences
- First violation: Warning + mandatory review removal
- Second violation: 30-day suspension
- Third violation: Permanent account termination

### 3. Emergency Protocols
- User can immediately block another user
- Report button for harassment/fraud
- 24/7 support contact for emergencies

# User Roles

## Overview

RuralConnect supports six primary user roles within its ecosystem, each with distinct capabilities and responsibilities.

## Role Definitions

### 1. Farmer
**Primary Need**: Access to equipment, labor, and services

**Capabilities**:
- Post work requests (plowing, sowing, harvesting, spraying)
- Search and book tractors, equipment, and services
- Hire laborers for agricultural tasks
- View quotes from service providers
- Manage active and past bookings
- Rate service providers
- Make payments

**Revenue Model**: Paying customer

---

### 2. Tractor Owner
**Primary Need**: Maximize equipment utilization and generate revenue

**Capabilities**:
- Register tractor(s) with specifications
- Set hourly/daily/seasonal rates
- Manage availability calendar
- View incoming work requests
- Accept/negotiate bookings
- Track work sessions
- Build reputation through ratings
- Receive payments

**Revenue Model**: Income from equipment rental

---

### 3. Contractor
**Primary Need**: Secure work and manage project teams

**Capabilities**:
- Browse available work requests
- Submit bids/quotes
- Hire and manage sub-contractors or laborers
- Schedule work and assign team members
- Track project progress
- Submit completion reports
- Receive payments
- Build portfolio and ratings

**Revenue Model**: Project-based income

---

### 4. Agricultural Laborer/Worker
**Primary Need**: Find paid work opportunities

**Capabilities**:
- Browse available work
- Apply for work opportunities
- Accept assigned work
- Log work hours/completion status
- Receive compensation
- Build work history and ratings
- Accept or decline work requests

**Revenue Model**: Hourly/daily wages

---

### 5. Equipment Owner (Other than Tractors)
**Primary Need**: Monetize equipment and maximize utilization

**Capabilities**:
- List equipment (harvesters, threshers, sprayers, etc.)
- Set rental rates
- Manage availability
- Receive and process bookings
- Track equipment usage
- Offer on-demand services (with operator)
- Receive payments
- Build reputation

**Revenue Model**: Equipment rental and service income

---

### 6. Agricultural Supplier
**Primary Need**: Reach rural customers directly

**Capabilities**:
- Create product catalog (seeds, fertilizers, tools, pesticides)
- Manage inventory
- Set prices and manage offers
- Process orders
- Manage logistics and shipping
- Track deliveries
- Receive payments
- Build customer reviews and reputation

**Revenue Model**: Product sales with potential wholesale discounts

---

## Role Transitions

- Farmers may also be equipment owners
- Contractors may also be laborers or equipment owners
- Users can hold multiple roles simultaneously

## Verification Requirements

| Role | Verification Needed |
|------|-------------------|
| Farmer | Land records / Aadhaar |
| Tractor Owner | Vehicle registration + Aadhaar |
| Contractor | License + Aadhaar |
| Laborer | Aadhaar + Reference |
| Equipment Owner | Equipment proof + Aadhaar |
| Supplier | Business registration + GST |
